Introduction

What is Braces Treatment?

Orthodontics is a specialized branch of dentistry focused on diagnosing, preventing, and treating dental and facial irregularities. Braces treatment is the most common orthodontic method used to straighten crooked teeth, align the jaw, and correct an improper bite.

A perfectly aligned smile isn't just about aesthetics; it is essential for long-term oral health. Crooked and crowded teeth are difficult to clean, making them highly susceptible to decay and gum disease. By applying gentle, continuous pressure over time, braces slowly move teeth into their ideal positions.

Types of Braces Available

We offer a variety of advanced orthodontic treatments tailored to your unique smile goals, lifestyle, and clinical needs.

Traditional Metal Braces

The most common and effective option. Uses high-grade stainless steel brackets and archwires to straighten teeth and correct complex bite issues.

Highly Durable Maximum Control Cost-Effective

Best For: Children, Teens, Complex Cases

VisibilityHigh
ComfortModerate
Complexity HandlingExcellent
MaintenanceHigh

Ceramic Braces

Functions just like metal braces but uses clear or tooth-colored ceramic brackets that blend in naturally with your teeth.

Tooth-Colored Discreet Look Effective Alignment

Best For: Older Teens & Adults

VisibilityModerate
ComfortModerate
Complexity HandlingExcellent
MaintenanceHigh (Stains easily)

Self-Ligating Braces

Uses a specialized slide mechanism or clip instead of elastic ties to hold the wire, reducing friction and pressure on the teeth.

No Elastics Less Friction Faster Visits Easier to Clean

Best For: Patients seeking comfort & speed

VisibilityHigh (if metal)
ComfortHigh
Complexity HandlingExcellent
MaintenanceLow

Clear Aligners

Virtually invisible, custom-made plastic trays that you can remove to eat, brush, and floss. Slowly shifts teeth without wires.

Nearly Invisible Removable No Diet Restrictions

Best For: Adults & Teens with mild/moderate issues

VisibilityVery Low
ComfortExcellent
Complexity HandlingLimited
MaintenanceHigh (must wear 22hrs/day)
