What is a Dental Filling?

A dental filling is a safe, effective restorative treatment designed to repair minor to moderate tooth damage caused by decay (cavities) or physical trauma. By meticulously removing the damaged portion and filling the space with advanced, durable materials, we can restore the tooth's original shape, strength, and function.

At CareFirst Dental Clinic, we prioritize minimally invasive dentistry. This means our tooth-colored fillings not only blend seamlessly with your natural smile but also require less removal of your healthy tooth structure compared to older silver amalgam fillings. The result is a strong, comfortable restoration that looks exactly like your natural tooth.

When Might You Need a Filling?

Tooth Decay

Repairing cavities and sealing the tooth to prevent harmful bacteria from reaching the sensitive inner nerve.

Chipped or Cracked Teeth

Restoring minor structural damage caused by trauma or biting on hard objects, protecting the tooth from further stress.

Worn Teeth

Rebuilding areas of the tooth enamel that have been worn away by chronic teeth grinding (bruxism) or acid erosion.

WATCH FOR THESE

Signs & Symptoms

Cavities don't always cause pain immediately. Look out for these warning signs:

  • Tooth Sensitivity: Pain when eating sweet, hot, or cold foods.
  • Pain While Eating: A sharp ache when you bite down.
  • Visible Holes: Pits or holes you can see or feel with your tongue.
  • Dark Spots: Black, brown, or white staining on the tooth surface.
  • Food Traps: Food constantly getting stuck in the exact same spot.
THE ROOT OF THE PROBLEM

Causes of Tooth Decay

Decay is a process that happens over time. Common culprits include:

  • Poor Oral Hygiene: Inadequate brushing leaves plaque behind.
  • Plaque & Bacteria: Bacteria convert sugars into enamel-destroying acid.
  • Sugary Drinks & Foods: Frequent snacking fuels acid production.
  • Acidic Foods: Citrus and sodas actively erode protective enamel.
  • Skipping Check-ups: Missing professional cleanings allows plaque to harden into tartar.

Filling Materials Tailored to Your Smile

Composite (Tooth-Colored)

The most popular choice. Made from a resin that perfectly matches the color of your natural teeth. Ideal for visible areas and front teeth.

  • Natural appearance
  • Strong & durable
  • Mercury-free

Glass Ionomer

Releases fluoride to help protect the tooth from further decay. Commonly used for cavities near the gum line or for children's teeth.

  • Fluoride releasing
  • Good for gum line
  • Ideal for kids

Amalgam (Silver)

Highly durable but very noticeable. Often used for back molars that endure heavy chewing forces (Available upon request/clinical need).

  • Extremely durable
  • Long-lasting
  • Ideal for back teeth

After Your Filling: Simple Care Tips

Wait Until Numbness Wears Off

Avoid chewing on the numb side of your mouth to prevent accidentally biting your lip, cheek, or tongue.

Mild Sensitivity Is Normal

Temporary sensitivity to hot or cold foods may occur for a few days but should gradually subside.

Keep the Tooth Clean

Brush and floss normally around the new filling to maintain its longevity and prevent future decay.
