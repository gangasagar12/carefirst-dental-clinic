Introduction

What is a Digital Dental X-Ray?

A digital dental X-ray is an advanced imaging technique that uses electronic sensors instead of traditional photographic film to capture images of your teeth, roots, and jawbone. These images are instantly projected onto a computer screen, allowing our dentists to diagnose oral problems with incredible precision.

Why is a Dental X-Ray Important?

Many dental issues—like infections in the root, bone loss, or cavities between teeth—are entirely invisible to the naked eye during a routine clinical exam. X-rays are the only way to accurately diagnose these hidden problems early, preventing them from developing into severe emergencies.

Indications

When Do You Need an X-Ray?

We rely on digital imaging for various treatments and diagnostics, including:

  • Routine Check-ups: To monitor ongoing oral health.
  • Tooth Pain: Identifying the hidden source of discomfort.
  • Root Canal & Extractions: Assessing root structure beforehand.
  • Implants & Orthodontics: Detailed planning for complex treatments.

Imaging Types

Types of Digital X-Rays

Periapical (PA) X-Ray

Captures the entire tooth from the crown down to the very tip of the root and surrounding bone. Crucial for root canals and deep infections.

Bitewing X-Ray

Shows details of the upper and lower teeth in one area. Perfect for detecting cavities forming between the back teeth.

Panoramic X-Ray

Provides a wide, complete view of your entire mouth, jaw, and teeth in a single image. Used for orthodontics and implant planning.

Advantages & Safety

Benefits & Radiation Safety

Ultra-Low Radiation

Digital sensors require up to 90% less radiation than traditional film X-rays, making them incredibly safe.

Instant Results

No waiting for film to develop. High-resolution images appear on our monitors instantly.

Enhanced Image Quality

We can zoom in, adjust contrast, and colorize the images to detect the tiniest of cavities.

Protective Measures

We always provide lead aprons and thyroid collars to ensure zero unnecessary exposure.
