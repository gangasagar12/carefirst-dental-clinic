What is General Dentistry?

General dentistry involves the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of a wide variety of conditions, disorders, and diseases affecting the teeth, gums, and maxillofacial region of the body. It serves as your first line of defense against oral health issues.

At CareFirst Dental Clinic, we focus heavily on preventive care—helping you maintain optimal oral health before problems arise. From routine cleanings and digital X-rays to restorative fillings and personalized hygiene advice, our goal is to ensure your natural teeth remain healthy and pain-free for a lifetime.

Why Regular Check-ups Matter

Early Detection

Identify dental concerns, such as hidden cavities or early signs of gum disease, before they become serious and require complex treatment.

Preventive Care

Maintain healthy teeth and gums through routine professional cleanings, removing stubborn plaque and tartar that daily brushing misses.

Long-Term Savings

Addressing oral health problems early often reduces the need for extensive, expensive, and time-consuming future dental treatments.

Common Problems We Treat

Tooth Decay

Treating cavities safely and restoring the structural integrity of your natural tooth.

Gum Inflammation

Addressing red, swollen, or bleeding gums to halt the progression of periodontal disease.

Tooth Sensitivity

Diagnosing and treating sharp pain caused by hot, cold, or sweet foods and beverages.

Plaque & Tartar Build-up

Professional removal of hardened deposits to prevent bad breath and gum disease.

Stained Teeth

Removing surface stains through professional polishing to reveal a cleaner, brighter smile.

Early Cavities

Detecting micro-cavities with digital imaging and treating them before they cause pain.
