Our Story

CareFirst Dental Clinic was founded with a simple belief: every patient deserves respectful, ethical, and personalized dental care.

What began as a vision to create a patient-first dental practice has grown into a trusted multispecialty clinic serving families across Shankhamul, New Baneshwor, and surrounding areas of Kathmandu. Led by Dr. Subash Banjade and supported by senior consultant doctors, CareFirst combines professional expertise with compassionate care, helping patients feel informed, comfortable, and confident throughout their dental journey.
