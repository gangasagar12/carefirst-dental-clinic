{% trans "What is a" %} {% trans "Digital Dental X-Ray?" %}

{% trans "A digital dental X-ray is an advanced imaging technique that uses electronic sensors instead of traditional photographic film to capture images of your teeth, roots, and jawbone. These images are instantly projected onto a computer screen, allowing our dentists to diagnose oral problems with incredible precision." %}

{% trans "Why is a Dental X-Ray Important?" %}

{% trans "Many dental issues—like infections in the root, bone loss, or cavities between teeth—are entirely invisible to the naked eye during a routine clinical exam. X-rays are the only way to accurately diagnose these hidden problems early, preventing them from developing into severe emergencies." %}

{% trans "Types of Digital X-Rays" %}

{% trans "Periapical (PA) X-Ray" %}

{% trans "Captures the entire tooth from the crown down to the very tip of the root and surrounding bone. Crucial for root canals and deep infections." %}

{% trans "Bitewing X-Ray" %}

{% trans "Shows details of the upper and lower teeth in one area. Perfect for detecting cavities forming between the back teeth." %}

{% trans "Panoramic X-Ray" %}

{% trans "Provides a wide, complete view of your entire mouth, jaw, and teeth in a single image. Used for orthodontics and implant planning." %}
