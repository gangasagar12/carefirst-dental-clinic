{% trans "What is a" %} {% trans "Digital Dental X-Ray?" %}

{% blocktrans %}A digital dental X-ray is an advanced diagnostic imaging technology that utilizes ultra-sensitive electronic sensors instead of traditional chemical film. It captures crystal-clear images of your teeth, roots, and jawbone instantly on our HD clinical monitors with zero waiting time.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Why are Dental X-Rays Essential?" %}

{% blocktrans %}Over 50% of serious dental issues—such as root canal infections, bone loss from gum disease, and interdental cavities—develop beneath the surface where visual inspection cannot reach. Digital radiography enables early detection before severe pain or tooth loss occurs.{% endblocktrans %}
