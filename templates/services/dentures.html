{% trans "What Are Dentures?" %}

{% trans "Dentures are custom-made, removable dental appliances designed to replace missing teeth and surrounding tissues. When you lose teeth due to severe decay, periodontal disease, or trauma, your facial muscles can sag, making you look older. Dentures fill out the appearance of your face and profile." %}

At Carefirst Dental Clinic, we craft modern dentures that are comfortable, functional, and look incredibly natural. Whether you are missing a few teeth or an entire arch, we provide personalized solutions to help you eat, speak, and smile with confidence again.

{% trans "Who Needs Dentures?" %}
  • {% trans "Patients who have lost several or all of their teeth." %}
  • {% trans "Individuals seeking an affordable alternative to dental implants." %}
  • {% trans "Those who have experienced significant jaw bone loss and cannot support implants." %}
{% trans "Custom Solutions" %}

{% trans "Types of Dentures" %}

{% trans "We offer different types of dentures depending on how many teeth you need replaced. Our team will help you choose the best option during your consultation." %}

{% trans "Removable Partial Denture (RPD)" %}

{% trans "Designed for patients who still have some healthy natural teeth remaining in their upper or lower jaw." %}

  • {% trans "Fills in the spaces created by missing teeth." %}
  • {% trans "Prevents other teeth from shifting out of position." %}
  • {% trans "Often features a metal framework or flexible acrylic base with clasps that attach to your natural teeth for stability." %}

{% trans "Complete Denture (CD)" %}

{% trans "Designed for patients who have lost all of their teeth in a full dental arch (upper, lower, or both)." %}

  • {% trans "Restores the entire smile and facial structure." %}
  • {% trans "Custom-fitted acrylic base rests directly on the gums." %}
  • {% trans "Upper dentures cover the roof of the mouth for suction, while lower dentures are shaped like a horseshoe to accommodate the tongue." %}

{% trans "Maintenance" %}

{% trans "Denture Care Tips" %}

• {% trans "Clean Daily" %}

{% trans "Brush your dentures daily with a soft-bristled brush and non-abrasive denture cleanser (not regular toothpaste) to remove food and plaque." %}

• {% trans "Remove at Night" %}

{% trans "Always remove your dentures before sleeping to give your gums a chance to rest and prevent fungal infections." %}

• {% trans "Keep Them Moist" %}

{% blocktrans %}When not wearing them, store your dentures in water or a mild denture-soaking solution so they don't dry out and lose their shape.{% endblocktrans %}

• {% trans "Handle with Care" %}

{% trans "Dentures are delicate and can break if dropped. Always clean them over a folded towel or a basin of water." %}
