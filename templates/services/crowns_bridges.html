{% trans "What Are" %} {% trans "Crowns & Bridges?" %}

{% trans "Crowns and bridges are fixed prosthetic devices cemented onto existing teeth or implants. Unlike dentures, which you can take out and clean daily, crowns and bridges can only be removed by a dentist." %}

{% trans "Dental Crowns" %}

{% blocktrans %}A crown (or \"cap\") completely covers a damaged, weak, or severely decayed tooth to restore its original shape, size, strength, and appearance. It is typically used after a root canal or when a large cavity threatens the health of the tooth.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Dental Bridges" %}

{% trans "A bridge is used to replace one or more missing teeth. It consists of an artificial tooth (pontic) anchored between two crowns placed on the healthy teeth (abutments) on either side of the gap." %}
