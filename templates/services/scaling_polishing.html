{% trans "What is" %} Scaling & Polishing?

{% blocktrans %}Scaling and polishing (often referred to simply as a \"dental cleaning\") is a professional procedure designed to thoroughly clean the teeth and gums. Even with the best at-home brushing and flossing routine, plaque can accumulate in hard-to-reach areas and harden into tartar (calculus)—a substance so tough it can only be removed by a dental professional.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Why It Is Important" %}

{% trans "Left untreated, plaque and tartar harbor bacteria that irritate the gums, leading to gingivitis and eventually advanced periodontal (gum) disease. Professional cleaning is the single most effective way to prevent these issues, halt bone loss, and keep your natural teeth for a lifetime." %}
