{% trans "What is Braces Treatment?" %}

{% trans "Orthodontics is a specialized branch of dentistry focused on diagnosing, preventing, and treating dental and facial irregularities. Braces treatment is the most common orthodontic method used to straighten crooked teeth, align the jaw, and correct an improper bite." %}

{% blocktrans %}A perfectly aligned smile isn't just about aesthetics; it is essential for long-term oral health. Crooked and crowded teeth are difficult to clean, making them highly susceptible to decay and gum disease. By applying gentle, continuous pressure over time, braces slowly move teeth into their ideal positions.{% endblocktrans %}
