{% trans "What Are" %} {% trans "Dental Implants?" %}

{% trans "A dental implant is a small, bio-compatible titanium post that is surgically placed into your jawbone beneath the gum line. It acts as an artificial tooth root, providing a permanent and incredibly strong foundation for a replacement tooth (crown) or a bridge." %}

{% trans "Because titanium fuses naturally with human bone (a process called" %} {% trans "osseointegration" %}{% trans "), implants do not slip, make noise, or cause bone damage the way dentures or conventional bridges might. They look, feel, and function exactly like natural teeth." %}

{% trans "Components of an Implant" %}

{% trans "1. The Fixture (Implant Post)" %}

{% trans "The titanium screw placed directly into the jawbone. It replaces the natural root." %}

{% trans "2. The Abutment" %}

{% trans "A small connector piece attached to the top of the implant post once it has healed. It holds the new tooth securely." %}

{% trans "3. The Crown" %}

{% trans "The custom-made artificial tooth (usually Zirconia or Porcelain) that sits on top of the abutment, perfectly matching your natural teeth." %}

{% trans "Candidacy" %}

{% trans "Who Is a Good Candidate?" %}

{% trans "Most people who are healthy enough to undergo a routine dental extraction are good candidates for implants. You need:" %}

  • {% trans "Healthy Gums:" %} {% trans "Free from periodontal disease." %}
  • {% trans "Adequate Bone:" %} {% trans "Enough jawbone to anchor the implant (or willing to undergo a bone graft)." %}
  • {% trans "Commitment:" %} {% trans "Dedication to excellent oral hygiene and regular dental visits." %}
{% trans "Note:" %} {% trans "Heavy smokers, people suffering from uncontrolled chronic disorders (like diabetes), or patients who have had radiation therapy to the head/neck area need evaluation on an individual basis." %}
{% trans "Making the Choice" %}

{% trans "Why Choose Implants Over Bridges or Dentures?" %}

{% trans "Preserves Natural Teeth" %}

{% trans "Unlike dental bridges, an implant does not require the dentist to grind down or damage the healthy adjacent teeth to support the new tooth." %}

{% trans "Prevents Bone Loss" %}

{% trans "When you lose a tooth, the jawbone underneath begins to melt away (resorb) due to lack of stimulation. Implants act like real roots, stimulating the bone and preventing facial collapse." %}

{% trans "Unmatched Stability" %}

{% trans "Dentures can slip, click, or fall out while speaking and eating. Implants are fixed permanently into the bone, allowing you to eat hard foods (like apples and steak) with complete confidence." %}

{% trans "Solutions" %}

{% trans "Types of Implant Solutions" %}

{% trans "Single Tooth Implant" %}

{% trans "Replaces one missing tooth completely independently, without affecting neighboring teeth." %}

{% trans "Implant-Supported Bridge" %}

{% trans "Used when multiple teeth are missing in a row. Instead of an implant for every tooth, two implants can support a bridge of 3 or 4 teeth." %}

{% trans "Full Arch (All-on-4 / All-on-6)" %}

{% trans "Replaces an entire jaw of missing teeth. 4 to 6 strategically placed implants support a full, permanent fixed bridge." %}

{% trans "The Procedure" %}

{% trans "Our Implant" %} {% trans "Treatment Process" %}

1. 3D Scan & Consultation

{% trans "We use a CBCT 3D scanner to assess your jawbone density, locate nerves, and digitally plan the precise placement of the implant." %}

{% trans "2. Surgical Placement" %}

{% trans "Under local anesthesia (totally painless), the titanium implant is gently placed into the jawbone. The gums are sutured back over it." %}

{% trans "3. Healing (Osseointegration)" %}

{% trans "Over the next 3 to 6 months, the jawbone naturally grows and fuses tightly around the titanium post, locking it firmly in place." %}

{% trans "4. Abutment Placement" %}

{% trans "Once healed, a small connector (abutment) is attached to the top of the implant to hold your new crown." %}

{% trans "5. Final Crown Placement" %}

{% trans "A custom-milled, perfectly shade-matched Zirconia or Porcelain crown is cemented onto the abutment. Your new tooth is complete!" %}
